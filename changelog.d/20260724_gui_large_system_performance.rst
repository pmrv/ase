GUI
---

- Large structures are much more responsive to interact with.  While
  dragging or zooming, bonds, labels and vectors are omitted and restored
  once interaction stops; atoms completely hidden behind nearer atoms are
  not drawn; and rubber-band selection no longer redraws the whole scene
  for every mouse movement.  Both new behaviours can be
  turned off under View -> Settings, and neither has any effect on
  structures small enough to already redraw quickly.
