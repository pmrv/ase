Bugfixes
--------

- :class:`~ase.thermochemistry.IdealGasThermo` (and other
  :class:`~ase.thermochemistry.BaseThermoChem` subclasses) now raise a
  ``ValueError`` when a negative ``spin`` is given. ``spin`` is the total
  electronic spin magnitude, so a negative value previously produced
  infinite or NaN electronic entropy via ``kB * log(2 * spin + 1)`` instead
  of a clear error (:issue:`1939`).
