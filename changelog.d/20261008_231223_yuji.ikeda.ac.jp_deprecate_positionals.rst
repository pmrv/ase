Optimizers
----------

- Deprecated positional arguments except :class:`~ase.Atoms` for
  :class:`~ase.optimize.optimize.Optimizer` subclasses.
  They should be passed as keyword arguments (:mr:`4280`)

Other changes
-------------

- Added :func:`~ase.utils.deprecate_positionals` to facilitate deprecation of
  positional arguments (:mr:`4280`)
